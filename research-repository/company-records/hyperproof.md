*Three-phase interior shape:*

*Phase 1 (Jan–Sep 2025): Gradual decline from Mar 2025 early-window peak (44,878) to pre-collapse level (~36–39k). Top positions (1-3) growing throughout — from 1,181 (Jan) to 3,026 (Sep) — masked by softening in the long tail (51+: 9,867 Jan → 5,521 Sep). Mid-range (11-20) holding.*

*Phase 2 (Oct–Nov 2025): Sudden collapse confined entirely to positions 11-50+. Positions 11-20: -60% Oct, -33% Nov. Positions 21-50: -72% Oct, -45% Nov. Positions 51+: -71% Oct, -43% Nov. Top positions (1-3 and 4-10) essentially flat or slightly declining — not the source of the traffic loss. Trough: 27,941 (Nov 2025).*

*Phase 3 (Dec 2025–Jun 2026): Recovery driven exclusively by top-position growth. Positions 1-3 grew from 3,002 (Nov) to 4,565 (Mar 2026); positions 4-10 from 5,289 to 6,403. Mid/long-tail remained suppressed throughout — 51+ at 16 (essentially zero) by Jun 2026. Recovery is continuation of pre-existing top-position momentum, not a rebound of the collapsed positions.*

*Window-end vs. peak: (45,989 − 50,450) / 50,450 = -8.8%. The Jun 2026 endpoint is 8.8% below the Mar 2026 all-time high but +26.78% above the window start and +64.5% above the Nov 2025 trough.*

## Risk Flags

- **No Search Console data:** Cannot confirm CTR trends, specific timing of ranking changes at the query level, or how AI Overview presence affected CTR on top-ranking pages.
- **Branded/non-branded split from intent data only:** Keyword export showed 0 branded keywords (likely a filter/configuration issue), so the branded/non-branded characterization rests entirely on the perf file's intent-based classification. Internally consistent and directionally clear, but not cross-validated against keyword export.
- **"Risk management frameworks" breakthrough mechanism unconfirmed:** The ranking jump from position 21 to position 8 on a 56k-volume keyword is the largest single traffic gain (+2,454), but the specific trigger (content update? backlink event? core update correction?) was not identified. The page has 50 referring domains — modest but real; no specific link event tied to the ranking improvement.
- **AI Observation Protocol not run:** No AI-tool citations checked. Priority queries documented in AI Discoverability section above.
- **Specific algorithm update not definitively identified:** The Oct-Nov 2025 collapse is assessed as Medium confidence for Algorithm Update causation; whether it is the Aug 2025 spam update tail or an October 2025 core update cycle is unresolved.
- **Pre-window branded spike cause unconfirmed:** Sep-Oct 2024 branded traffic spike (11,980-12,420) was not attributed to a specific event. Does not affect within-window analysis.
- **TV/traffic divergence (lower severity):** +10.9% TV vs. +26.78% traffic — the new traffic is skewed toward lower-CPC informational queries. Not a major flag given the overall commercial alignment of the content model, but worth monitoring.

## Narrative

### Company Overview

Hyperproof is a mid-market ($77.3M raised) B2B SaaS GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance) platform. Two significant in-window corporate events — the Hyperproof AI product launch (Sep 22, 2025) and the Expent.ai acquisition (Oct 7, 2025) — generated press coverage and backlinks but did not cause the traffic trajectory's main inflections. The Oct-Nov 2025 position collapse occurred in close temporal proximity to both events but is better explained by an algorithm update; both events would be expected to generate positive organic signals (backlinks, branded search), not ranking losses.

### Traffic Trajectory

Hyperproof's organic estimated traffic grew from 36,277 (January 2025) to 45,989 (June 2026), +26.78% net — but the interior trajectory was "Decline then Recovery." From the window open, traffic drifted down gradually to 44,878 (Mar 2025) then gradually declined further; in October 2025, mid/long-tail positions collapsed suddenly (-60% to -72% in positions 11-50+ in a single month) while top positions held. Trough: 27,941 (Nov 2025). Recovery onset: December 2025. March 2026 all-time historical high: 50,450. Window-end: 45,989 (-8.8% below peak, +26.78% above window start).

### Mechanism Analysis

**Recovery mechanism (High confidence):** The recovery is the continuation of pre-existing, uninterrupted top-position growth — not a rebound of the collapsed mid/long-tail positions, which remained near-zero through window-end. Positions 1-3 grew from 1,181 (Jan 2025) to 4,565 (Mar 2026) without a single reversal. The single largest contributing event was the "risk management frameworks" ranking breakthrough (position 21 → position 8, 56k volume, +2,454 traffic). New content launched around corporate events (AI Risk Management Frameworks guide, TPRM product page) contributed 111-176 RDs and additional traffic from press coverage.

**Collapse mechanism (Medium confidence):** The Oct-Nov 2025 position-bucket collapse (positions 11-50+ falling 60-72% in one month) is consistent with the August 2025 spam update (Aug 26-Sep 22) tail or October 2025 core update activity. No competing off-content explanation was found; the corporate events occurring simultaneously (AI launch, Expent acquisition) would generate positive, not negative, organic signals. Backlinks grew throughout the collapse, ruling out authority decay.

**The central finding — foundational vs. peripheral content concentration:** Hyperproof's losing pages are concentrated in event-driven topical content (Change Healthcare breach, EO 14028, NIST CSF 2.0 update), narrower regulatory niches (FedRAMP, DevSecOps), and cybersecurity-specific content distinct from Hyperproof's primary GRC positioning. What is absent from the losers: Hyperproof's foundational GRC definitional content (risk management techniques, cyber risk, segregation of duties, compliance auditing). These foundational pages held and grew — in direct contrast to Secureframe and Sprinto, where the equivalent foundational pages (what-is-soc-2, what-is-iso-27001) are among the primary losers.

**AIO Presence:** One directly confirmed AIO case: the IT Risk Management Framework page ranks position 8 for "risk management frameworks" (56k vol) with an AI Overview confirmed active (SERP features column) and 2,167 traffic generated — making this the first record in the repository with a direct keyword-export-confirmed AIO Presence Observed = Yes and AIO Observed Effect = Resistant.

### Client Applicability

Hyperproof's highest-value use case is as a diagnostic framework for traffic decline — specifically, the audit question: "Which of my declining pages are foundational to my primary service area, and which are peripheral?" Clients who find their foundational pages intact despite a traffic decline are in a recoverable position similar to Hyperproof's. Clients who find their foundational pages are the primary losers are in a structurally more serious position comparable to Secureframe's or Sprinto's.

The secondary use case is demonstrating that corporate events (product launches, acquisitions) can generate rapid link accumulation for specifically-timed new content — an actionable tactic for clients with upcoming announcements.
