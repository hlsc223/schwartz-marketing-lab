*Two-phase interior shape:*

*Phase 1 (Jan 2025–Dec 2025): Flat/stable, oscillating in the 67k-78k range throughout. The window-trough occurs Feb 2025 (67,483). *Phase 2 (Jan 2026–May 2026): Sharp, sustained acceleration from the ~67-78k range to the all-time high of 124,676 (May 2026). *Jun 2026: Slight pullback to 115,307 (-7.5% below peak). Window ends +60.3% above window start.*

*Note: The ranking-position-bucket collapse at long-tail positions (21-50 and 51+) precedes rather than coincides with the acceleration. ## Risk Flags

- **Causal link is timing-and-pattern correlation, not confirmed mechanism:** The exact timing of new GRC/product-page publication relative to the Nov 18, 2025 launch announcement is inferred from Ahrefs first-seen dates and traffic patterns, not confirmed via an internal publish-date log. The 1-2 month lag between launch and traffic acceleration is consistent with the hypothesis but not independently verified.
- **Two-page concentration risk:** The two largest pages (GRC/risk-frameworks page + homepage) account for ~68% of net Top Pages gain. If either loses rankings, the headline improvement is significantly exposed. The GRC page's position-1-9 range on "risk management frameworks" is healthy but not exclusively position-1; ranking attrition here would significantly affect the net.
- **Single large loser unresolved:** /collection/soc-2/what-is-soc-2 lost -11,233 traffic from a single keyword ("soc2") falling from position 2 to position 30. The trigger for this single-keyword ranking collapse was not identified (no redirect, no URL change; other keywords on the same page moved in both directions). This could recur on other pillar pages.
- **"Other brands" composition volatility unresolved:** The perf file's "Other brands" column showed unexplained volatility (7,778 Jan 2025 → 29,190 May 2026 → 11,134 Jun 2026). The source of this swing was not investigated (closed per the stopping test as unlikely to change the overall conclusion). This warrants investigation before external citation of composition figures.
- **Cannibalization rate not formally computed:** The standard Top Pages gross gain/gross loss computation was not run for Vanta. The Healthy-Moderate estimate is based on the net and the largest single loser, not a formal computation.
- **AIO Presence is directional only:** The keyword export SERP features column shows AI Overview tags on "risk management frameworks" query variants, but the scattered position range (1-9) makes this a weaker confirmation than Hyperproof's directly confirmed position-8 case. No CTR data available.
- **AI Observation Protocol not run:** No AI-tool citations checked. Priority queries documented above.
- **No Search Console data:** No CTR data, no impression data, no position confirmation at the query level.

## Narrative

### Company Overview

Vanta is a B2B SaaS compliance automation and GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance) platform, founded 2018, CEO Christina Cacioppo throughout. The company reached $300M ARR (threefold growth over two years), 1,000 employees, and 16,000+ customers by April 2026 and was named a Leader in the Forrester Wave for GRC Platforms, Q2 2026. Two in-window funding events: $150M Series D (July 23, 2025, $4.15B valuation) and the Agentic Trust Platform launch (November 18, 2025) — the latter is the primary causal candidate for the traffic trajectory.

### Traffic Trajectory

Organic estimated traffic grew from 71,941 (January 2025) to 115,307 (June 2026), +60.3% net. The interior shape is "flat, then accelerate" — traffic oscillated in the 67k-78k range for most of 2025, then accelerated sharply beginning January 2026, reaching an all-time high of 124,676 in May 2026, followed by a mild pullback (-7.5%) to the June 2026 window-close.

### Mechanism Analysis

**Primary mechanism (High confidence):** The Agentic Trust Platform launch (November 18, 2025), funded by the July 2025 $150M Series D, is the primary causal candidate. Three independent lines of evidence corroborate this:

**(1) Off-content evidence:** Multiple independent press sources confirm the November 18, 2025 launch (BusinessWire, SiliconANGLE, Yahoo Finance). A further AI Agent/enterprise-controls expansion was confirmed March 19, 2026. These are the best-corroborated off-content events in the study.

**(2) Content-level evidence:** New product pages (/products/risk, /products/grc, /products/ai, /products/automated-compliance, /products/third-party-risk-management) appear in Ahrefs data going from 0 to meaningful traffic in this window. The CMMC collection page was first seen September 2025. Compliance-framework entity tracking shows broad expansion: HITRUST +491%, FedRAMP new to 358, NIST +62%, HIPAA +354% — all consistent with a wave of new framework-specific content launched alongside or following the platform announcement.

**(3) Timing match:** Pass 2's own Notes field describes "flat/stable through most of 2025, then accelerated sharply starting Jan 2026" — a 1-2 month lag from the November 18, 2025 launch, consistent with Google's crawl-and-index-and-rank cycle.

Unlike Harness's funding-timing correlation (financial event with no confirmed content link), here the launched product directly maps to the observed content expansion. This is the most directly and best-corroborated causal story in the study's growth cohort.

**Secondary mechanism (within the same primary driver):** Branded traffic grew +38.1% alongside non-branded's +159.3% — both segments growing substantially simultaneously. The branded growth is plausibly the brand-visibility effect of the widely-covered November 2025 Agentic Trust Platform press coverage, which generated branded search demand in addition to new topical content. This is the first case in the study where branded and non-branded traffic grow together substantially, introducing a third composition shape to the taxonomy.

**AIO (directional):** The GRC/risk-management-frameworks page holds positions 1-9 across "risk management frameworks" query variants, most with AI Overview active per the keyword export SERP features column. Real traffic is generated at each position tier despite AI Overview presence — a fourth corroborating instance of GRC/definitional content resisting AI Overview suppression, though on weaker evidence (scattered positions 1-9) than Hyperproof's directly confirmed position-8 case.
