# Ebook Design System Card
## The Modern Organic Growth Playbook — Schwartz Marketing Lab

Use this as context when working in Canva or asking for design help.

---

## Report Identity

- **Title:** The Modern Organic Growth Playbook
- **Author/Brand:** Schwartz Marketing Lab (S.LAB)
- **Format:** 1920×1080px (16:9 widescreen), report-style — not a slide deck
- **Output:** PDF, screen-only (no print optimization needed)
- **Purpose:** B2B authority piece for VPs of Marketing, CMOs, and content strategists at B2B SaaS companies. Research-backed. Designed to be cited, shared on LinkedIn, and used in sales conversations.
- **Tone:** Intelligent, direct, evidence-first. Not consultancy-speak.

| Accent (cobalt) | `#4269e2` | Highlights, chapter numbers, key stats, links |
| Accent deep | `#2f53c7` | Hover states, borders, emphasis |

**Color philosophy:** Cream background with cobalt accent. Warm but precise. Avoid pure white as the primary background — use `#f4f1e7`. Reserve white for figure panels and callout insets.

## Page Types

Six templates to build in Canva before flowing content. Every page in the report maps to one of these.

### 1. Cover
Full-bleed design. Report title, author/brand, and a visual element. No body text. This is the only purely designed page — the rest follow a system.

### 2. Chapter Opener
Large chapter number (cobalt, oversized), chapter title below it, opening hook sentence in slightly smaller type. Minimal — mostly typographic. Feels like a chapter break in a book.

### 3. Body Text Page
The workhorse. Left ~60% column for text (heading + body paragraphs). Right ~40% either blank/white space, a pull quote, a small stat, or a sidebar label. Not every page needs content in the right column — breathing room is intentional.

### 4. Figure Page
Figure (PNG) takes the primary visual real estate — typically 60–75% of the page area. Figure number and title in caption style below or alongside. Optional 1–2 sentence context note. White or light panel behind the figure.

### 5. Callout Page
Three variants — all use large typography, minimal body text, and a left-side colored border or background panel:
- **Insight callout** (cobalt border): conceptual insight or framework introduction
- **Research synthesis** (neutral/cream panel): what the data showed across the cohort
- **Warning callout** (amber/red-tinted border): common misdiagnoses, what not to do

### 6. "Before You Move On" Summary Page
Closes each chapter. 3–5 bullet points summarizing the chapter's key takeaways. Simple layout. Chapter number in cobalt, summary heading, bulleted list. Acts as a navigational anchor for readers skimming.

## Design Principles

1. **One idea per page.** Every page should answer a single question for the reader.
2. **Report style, not slide style.** More text than a presentation, but visual hierarchy enforced by the widescreen canvas.
3. **White space is intentional.** Not every page needs to be full. Empty right columns are fine.
4. **Figures are the primary communication device.** When a figure is present, it should be large enough to read comfortably at full screen.
5. **Consistency over creativity.** Use the six page types. Don't invent new layouts mid-document.

## Design Starting Point

Build templates in this order:
1. Cover (designed fresh)
2. Chapter 4 body — this chapter contains all callout types, the decision tree figure, and standard body text. Build every template here before flowing other chapters.
3. Flow remaining chapters 1–3, 5–11 using the locked templates.
