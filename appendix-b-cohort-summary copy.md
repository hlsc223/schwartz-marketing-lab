# Appendix B: Cohort Summary

*The Modern Organic Growth Playbook: How the best B2B brands build durable demand in 2026*

---

This appendix summarizes the 118-company study cohort. For each company it reports category, traffic classification, traffic change, traffic trajectory, and Phase 3 analysis status.

Companies are grouped by category and sorted within each category by classification (Grew → Flat → Declined) and traffic change.

**Phase 3 analysis key:**
- ★ Deep dive — full 14-field mechanism analysis
- ◆ Comparison brief — structured paired comparison

Two companies (Semaphore and Kertos) began the study window with near-zero traffic, making percentage change mathematically unstable. Both are classified Grew based on the magnitude and direction of traffic acquired during the study window and therefore appear without a reported percentage. Semaphore remains a full-weight classifiable grower because page-level evidence supported confident assignment, while Kertos was excluded from the content growth-pattern distribution because its traffic base remained too small for defensible attribution.

See Appendix A and Appendix C for methodology and metric definitions.
