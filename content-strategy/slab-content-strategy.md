## Part 1: Modern Organic Growth Playbook — Distribution Plan

### Overview

The playbook is a research-backed report covering 18 months of B2B SaaS organic traffic data across 118 companies. The distribution strategy treats it as a launch event, not a one-time upload. Four channels: press release, indexed lead capture page, blog series, newsletter series.

---

### Channel 1: Press Release

**Angle:** New original research, not a product announcement.

The press release leads with the research findings — the 118-company study, the traffic split (47% declined, 37% grew), and the key differentiator (content type, not effort or scale). Position S.LAB as the organization that produced this research, not as an agency promoting its services.

**Headline options:**
- "New Research Across 118 B2B SaaS Companies Reveals What Separates Organic Traffic Winners from Losers in 2025–2026"
- "47% of B2B SaaS Companies Lost Organic Traffic in 2025. Study of 118 Companies Identifies Why."
- "B2B SaaS Organic Traffic Split Decisively in 2025. New Study Reveals the Differentiator Wasn't What Anyone Expected."

**Key data points to lead with:**
- 118 companies tracked across 18 months
- 47% declined, 37% grew — often dramatically, in the same categories
- The differentiator was content type, not domain authority, publishing volume, backlinks, or budget
- Five distinct mechanisms responsible for declines (Algorithm Update, AI Overview Interception, Architectural Damage, AI Tool Demand Substitution, Competitive Displacement)
- Companies with definitional/conceptual content showed structurally renewable demand; companies with satisfiable informational content showed fragility

**Target publications for direct pitch (beyond wire distribution):**
- Search Engine Land
- Search Engine Journal
- Marketing Brew
- MarTech
- Content Marketing Institute
- SparkToro blog
- The Growth Memo (Kevin Indig)
- Demand Curve
- Ahrefs Blog / Semrush Blog

**Distribution:** PRWeb, PR Newswire, or EIN Presswire for wire pickup. Simultaneously pitch the research angle directly to editors at the target publications above.

**Timing:** Release the day the lead capture page goes live. The press release should link directly to the lead capture page.

### Channel 2: Indexed Lead Capture Page

**URL:** `/playbook` or `/resources/modern-organic-growth-playbook`

**Why indexed matters:** A gated PDF with a thin landing page is invisible to search and AI systems. A full-content page with a form is a compounding asset — it ranks organically, gets cited in AI responses, and captures email in the same place. The page should stand alone as valuable content even without the download.

**What's gated vs. ungated:**

The core findings live on the page — ungated. The PDF is gated. This is the right architecture for both SEO and AEO: a substantive page ranks and gets cited by AI systems; a thin form page does neither. The PDF becomes valuable for its format and completeness, not because it's the only way to access the research.

- **Page (ungated):** Executive summary, methodology, key findings with real numbers, major charts (when available), chapter summaries, enough substance to stand alone as a research resource
- **PDF (gated):** Polished designed formatting, printable version, complete chapter text, additional charts, any worksheets or decision frameworks

**Page structure:**

```
H1: The Modern Organic Growth Playbook
Subhead: What 18 months of B2B SaaS traffic data reveals about which programs 
         survive the AI-era shift — and which don't.

--- Executive summary block ---
3-4 paragraphs covering the key findings in plain language
Written to be cited — this is the section AI systems and journalists will pull from

--- Key findings block ---
Data points with real numbers (118 companies, 47%/37% split, mechanism breakdown)

--- Methodology block ---
2-3 sentences on data sources, company selection criteria, study window
The credibility anchor for the research

--- Chapter summaries block ---
One substantive paragraph per chapter (not just a title list)
These are the AEO targets — specific questions each chapter answers

--- Who it's for block ---
- B2B SaaS marketing leaders diagnosing a traffic decline
- Growth teams trying to understand what's working and why
- Anyone building an organic program in an AI-affected search environment

--- Form block ---
First name / Work email / [Download the Full Playbook button]
Copy above form: "Get the complete designed report — plus future research 
updates as the study grows."

```

**SEO target queries for this page:**
- b2b organic growth research 2025
- b2b saas seo study
- organic traffic decline b2b
- ai overview impact b2b traffic
- modern organic growth playbook

**Schema:** `ScholarlyArticle` or `Book` schema on the page. Include `author`, `datePublished`, `about`, and `numberOfPages`.

## Part 2: Full Organic Content Strategy for S.LAB

### Strategic Foundation

S.LAB's content goal is qualified inbound lead generation — not traffic volume. Every piece of content should be evaluated against: does this attract someone with a real organic growth problem who has budget to solve it?

Every major content asset should originate from original research, client evidence, or a reusable decision framework. Commentary alone is not a durable competitive advantage.

The ongoing research program gives S.LAB something very few agencies have: original, verified, longitudinal data about organic growth patterns across a large B2B SaaS sample. That research is what the content expresses — the findings, the frameworks, and the decision support. Everything else should amplify it.

---

### Authority Areas S.LAB Can Own

These are the areas where S.LAB has genuine information advantage over competitors — places where the content can be the most credible, evidence-backed answer available.

**1. B2B Organic Growth Research**
The 118-company study is unique. No other agency has published longitudinal traffic analysis at this scale with this methodology. The research repository will continue to grow. This positions S.LAB as a research organization that does consulting, not a consulting firm that writes blog posts.

Ownable claim: *The most rigorous original research on what drives B2B organic growth outcomes.*

**2. AEO — Answer Engine Optimization**
S.LAB already published one of the most visible AEO resources (Top 11 AEO Agencies). The category is nascent, search volume is growing fast, and most content covering it is thin or written by general SEO agencies without specialization. Early mover advantage is real here.

Ownable claim: *The definitive practitioner resource on AEO strategy for B2B companies.*

**3. AI Discoverability & GEO**
The Scrunch citation study gives S.LAB actual data on what AI systems cite and why — not opinion. This is rare. Most GEO content is speculative. S.LAB can publish evidence-based recommendations, which are more credible and more likely to be cited by the AI systems themselves.

Ownable claim: *Evidence-backed guidance on getting found in AI search environments.*

**4. Mechanism-Based Organic Diagnosis**
The five-mechanism diagnostic framework (Ch 4 of the playbook) is S.LAB's proprietary IP. Nobody else has published this. It's actionable, defensible, and directly maps to the service offering — because the next step after diagnosis is hiring someone to fix the mechanism.

Ownable claim: *The clearest framework for diagnosing what's actually driving an organic traffic problem.*

**5. Durable Demand / Content Durability**
S.LAB's framework for building durable organic demand — the durability tier system and the two-question test — is proprietary IP with no direct equivalent in existing agency positioning. It's grounded in the research findings rather than invented as a positioning concept, which makes it defensible.

Ownable claim: *The framework for building organic content that compounds rather than decays.*
