# Validation Checklist: Evergreen Search Asset
*Run this after Article Strategy is approved, before starting the Pre-Writing Brief.*
*Target: 45–60 minutes. Search intent is the foundation — don't skip this.*

---

## 1. Keyword Validation

Confirm the primary keyword and supporting keywords from the Article Strategy.

- What is the primary keyword? What is its estimated monthly search volume?
- Are there adjacent keywords worth capturing in the same article?
- What is the most common natural language phrasing? (Check Google Suggest, PAA, Reddit, communities)
- Are there keyword variants we should avoid because they signal different intent?

## 2. Search Intent Audit

What is the reader actually trying to accomplish when they search this term?

- Informational (learn something)?
- Navigational (find something specific)?
- Commercial (evaluate options)?
- Transactional (take action)?

Review the top 5–10 results and identify:
- What format dominates? (listicle, guide, comparison, definition)
- What length dominates?
- What angle dominates?
- What does the current best result get right?
- What does it get wrong or miss?

## 4. AI Landscape Review

What are AI systems currently saying about this topic?

Run 1–2 prompts across **ChatGPT** and **Perplexity**.

- What is the current AI-generated answer?
- Is it accurate? Complete? Shallow?
- What sources does it cite?
- What would make our article more citable than what's currently being retrieved?

## 5. Differentiation Check

Given everything above — what is S.LAB's specific angle?

- Are we writing the same article as the current top result, or something meaningfully different?
- Do we have evidence, research, or experience that strengthens the answer beyond what exists?
- What can only S.LAB credibly contribute?

If the answer is "we'd write a similar article but better written" — that's not enough. Identify a specific differentiation before proceeding.

## GO / NO-GO

**Can this article realistically become one of the best resources on this topic — and hold that position over time?**

- [ ] **GO** — Clear search demand, realistic differentiation, evidence strengthens the answer. Proceed to Pre-Writing Brief.
- [ ] **NO-GO** — Keyword too competitive, differentiation too thin, or search intent doesn't match our angle. Reposition or choose a different article.
