# Validation Checklist: Opinion / POV
*Run this after Article Strategy is approved, before starting the Pre-Writing Brief.*
*Target: 15–20 minutes. The argument is the foundation — validate it before writing.*

---

## 1. Argument Stress Test

State the central argument in one sentence.

- Is this a genuinely held position, or a contrarian take for its own sake?
- What is the strongest counterargument? Can we address it honestly in the piece?
- Does S.LAB have direct evidence, observations, or experience that supports this position — or is it purely assertion?

## 2. Audience Readiness Check

Is the intended audience ready to engage with this argument?

- Have we published enough foundational content for readers to have the context this opinion requires?
- If not, should this piece be delayed until foundational articles (Five Mechanisms, Durable Demand, etc.) are live?
- Does the reader need to trust S.LAB's research before accepting this argument? If yes, is that trust established?

## 3. Current Conversation Check

What is the current state of this debate or topic?

- Is this a well-worn argument or a genuinely underexplored position?
- Who else has taken this position? Are we adding to the conversation or repeating it?
- Is there a recent event, data point, or development that makes this the right moment to publish?

## 4. Evidence Inventory

What evidence supports this position?

- Original research (preferred)
- Client observations
- Industry data
- Logical argument

An opinion piece without any supporting evidence is weaker than one grounded in observation. Identify what's available before writing.

## GO / NO-GO

**Does this argument add something to the conversation that S.LAB is specifically positioned to say — and that readers are ready to receive?**

- [ ] **GO** — Genuine position, supportable with evidence, audience is ready. Proceed to Pre-Writing Brief.
- [ ] **NO-GO** — Argument is thin, not the right moment, or foundational content needs to exist first. Delay or reframe.
