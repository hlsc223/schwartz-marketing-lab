# Validation Checklist: Research Publication
*Run this after Article Strategy is approved, before starting the Pre-Writing Brief.*
*Target: 20–30 minutes. This is validation, not research.*

---

## 1. Terminology Check

Are we using the language people actually use, or an internal framing that doesn't map to how readers search or talk?

Check: Google Suggest, "People Also Ask," and the first page of results for the core concept.

- What terms does the SERP use that differ from our working title?
- Is "organic traffic" the right phrase, or do people say "SEO traffic," "search traffic," etc.?
- Are there obvious phrases worth incorporating naturally into the H1 or meta — without changing the thesis?

## 2. SERP Review

What currently ranks for the primary keyword or closest equivalent?

- Are the top results listicles, opinion pieces, or other research publications?
- Is the field already crowded with authoritative research, or is there a clear gap?
- Does our article's angle meaningfully differ from what's already there?

## 3. AI Landscape Review

What are AI systems currently saying about this topic?

Run 1–2 prompts across **ChatGPT** and **Perplexity**. Check for an **AI Overview** on the primary keyword if applicable.

Ask:
- What is already well explained by AI systems?
- What is missing, incomplete, or incorrect in AI-generated answers?
- What would AI systems cite if our article existed? Is our content in that extractable format?

This matters because Research Publications target AI citations as a primary success metric. Understanding what AI systems currently say tells you what you're writing against and where the citation opportunity is.

- [ ] **GO** — The gap is real. Our evidence is unique. Proceed to Pre-Writing Brief.
- [ ] **NO-GO** — The gap is smaller than expected, or our contribution isn't differentiated enough. Reposition or choose a different article.
