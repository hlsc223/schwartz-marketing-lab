## Key stats to use (from stat-bank.md)

Pull these — don't paraphrase loosely, use the exact figures:

- **118** companies tracked, **10** verticals, **18-month** study window (Jan 2025–June 2026)
- **47%** declined, **16%** flat, **37%** grew — headline split
- **27%** of decliners lost >50% of their traffic; **30%** of all companies are currently >50% below their all-time peak
- Most common trajectory: **Early Peak then Decline** — 28 of 118 companies
- **61%** of full-weight classifiable growing companies grew primarily through definitional/educational content
- Aha!: held or improved rankings on 20+ queries while traffic fell **80–99%** on the same pages
- Aha! template content specific stat: Creative brief template pages −96.4%; Strategy templates −89.8%
- Braze: +59.9%; no single page exceeded ~15–18% of gains (distributed model)
- ZoomInfo: +154.7% traffic, +14.7% traffic value — 140-point divergence (traffic as misleading signal)

## Argument structure (proposed)

**1. Open with the finding, not the study.** Don't lead with "we studied 118 companies." Lead with what the data showed — the split. The study is the credential, not the hook.

**2. The numbers: what actually happened.** 47/16/37 split. Distribution understates divergence — companies at the extremes, not the middle. Same categories, opposite outcomes.

**3. Three forces, not one.** This is the key diagnostic reframe. Most people called it "AI" and treated it as one problem. Three distinct mechanisms, three distinct data signatures, three distinct responses. Name them:
   - Algorithm updates (August 2025 spam update — broad position collapse)
   - AI Overview interception (rankings held, traffic fell 80–99%)
   - AI tool demand substitution (search volume itself declined, not just CTR)

**4. What determined which side of the split a program landed on.** Content type — specifically whether it required continued engagement or satisfied a one-time query. This is where Durable Demand vs. Structural Fragility gets introduced.

**5. Two case studies.** 
   - **Braze** (the durable model): +59.9%, broad distributed educational content, no single page dominant, held through the environment
   - **Aha!** (the fragile model): mature program, extensive investment, −42.4% because template content was exactly what the three forces targeted. Rankings held. Traffic didn't. Rankings ≠ Traffic.

**6. The implication: your traffic number is not the diagnosis.** Traffic is a symptom. The mechanism is the diagnosis. Same traffic number, completely different prognosis depending on what's underneath it.

**7. CTA.** The full analysis is in the playbook. Link, brief description of what's in it, download CTA. One clean paragraph, not a list.
