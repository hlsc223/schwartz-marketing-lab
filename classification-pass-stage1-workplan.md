## Methodology Decisions (locked)

**Five content buckets:**
- **DE** — Definitional/educational (conceptual guides, methodology content, glossaries, frameworks)
- **T** — Tools/free resources (interactive tools, calculators, templates requiring ongoing engagement)
- **P** — Programmatic/volume (AI-generated or near-AI content at scale, data page networks, thin volume content)
- **B** — Brand/product-demand (primarily navigational/brand traffic — product adoption driving organic, not content program)
- **M** — Mixed — used ONLY when multiple drivers are genuinely and evidentially co-primary; not a placeholder for uncertainty

**Three classification statuses:**
- **Classifiable** — bucket assignable from existing evidence (web research + deep dive); no Ahrefs blocking
- **Needs Ahrefs** — dominant-driver uncertain; Ahrefs pull required before final assignment; dominant-driver hypothesis provided
- **Unclassifiable / Exclude** — unit-of-analysis problem, near-zero base making percentage meaningless, or insufficient data regardless of Ahrefs output; exclude from bucket distribution, footnote if needed

**Burden-of-proof rules:**
- For large established brands (BILL, SAI360, established enterprise platforms): default assumption = **B**; Ahrefs is there to test whether content-driven growth can be confirmed, not to reopen classification from zero
- "Has educational content on site" is not evidence that the content program was the organic growth engine
- M requires confirmed co-primary evidence — not just "has blog + another asset"
- For high-priority Stage 2 cases (Apollo, Notion, LaunchDarkly, SpendHound, Celoxis): forced resolution to dominant driver required; M only if evidence genuinely supports co-primary split

**Absolute traffic context required for:**
- Velaris, SpendHound, TeamSupport, Kertos (if pulled), Semaphore
- If ending absolute traffic is negligible despite large percentage growth, flag as low-weight evidence

## Part 4: Stage 2 Methodology

### What to pull (per company)

For each "Needs Ahrefs" company, request from Quinn: **Ahrefs top organic pages export** (top 50-100 pages by estimated monthly traffic, organic traffic only, same study window end date).

**For branded vs. non-branded split cases (Attentive, BILL, SAI360):** Also request traffic breakdown by query type or keywords driving the top pages — specifically to see what share is navigational/branded.

**For absolute scale cases (Velaris, SpendHound, TeamSupport, Semaphore):** Request starting-window traffic figure (Jan 2025 or earliest available) alongside the current figure, so absolute scale is visible alongside the percentage.

### Apollo resolution framework (DECISION-POINT)

Stage 2 output for Apollo must explicitly answer:

1. **Editorial/educational share:** What percentage of top-50 pages by traffic are sales methodology guides, educational blog posts, or content requiring genuine engagement?
2. **Programmatic data-page share:** What percentage are B2B contact/people search pages, company data pages, or other programmatic data assets?
3. **Brand/navigational share:** What percentage of top-traffic queries are branded or navigational?

Report the split as estimated percentages of traffic in the study window. Then assign:
- If editorial dominates (>50% of traffic) → **DE**
- If data pages dominate (>50%) → **P** (with note: programmatic data pages, distinct from ZoomInfo's thin content pattern)
- If brand/navigational dominates (>50%) → **B**
- If genuinely split across two co-primary mechanisms → **M** (with explicit split documented)

### Forced dominant-driver rules

For Notion, LaunchDarkly, SpendHound, Celoxis, Higher Logic, Amplemarket, Instantly.ai:

- Identify the top-3 pages by traffic. What content type are they?
- If 2 of 3 are the same type → that type is dominant → assign that bucket
- If top-3 genuinely split across two types, examine the top-10. If still split → M is defensible; document the split explicitly

### Low-weight evidence flags

After Stage 2, flag any company where:
- Starting absolute traffic was under ~5K monthly visits and ending traffic is under ~20K monthly visits → "low-weight evidence"
- Growth then Pullback trajectory AND the pullback recovered more than 50% of the gain → "durability unconfirmed"
