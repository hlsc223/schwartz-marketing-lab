# How to Read This Report

---

This report is designed to be read in full sequence. That's the recommended path — each chapter builds on the one before it, and the argument compounds.

If your situation is specific, the paths below identify the chapters most directly relevant to where you are right now.

---

**If your traffic is declining:**
Read Chapters 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 7 → 8 → 11.

Start here regardless of what you think caused the decline. Chapter 3 establishes whether you're reading the right number, and Chapter 4 identifies which mechanism is operating. The mechanism determines which of the Chapter 7 archetypes applies — or whether Chapter 8 is the more honest answer.

---

**If your traffic is growing:**
Read Chapters 1 → 2 → 3 → 5 → 6 → 11.

Growth is the condition where the most important risks are invisible. Chapters 2 and 3 are worth reading before the anatomy chapters — they establish the assumptions and measurement errors most likely to produce a false sense of security in a growing program.

---

**If AI visibility is your main concern:**
Read Chapters 1 → 2 → 3 → 9 → 10 → 11.

Chapter 9 covers what AI Overviews actually do to traffic at the query level. Chapter 10 covers how chatbot AI systems decide what to cite. Together, they establish the evidence base for the investment decisions in Chapter 11.

---

**If you're building a new content strategy:**
Read the full report in order.

The full sequence is roughly six hours of reading. The diagnostic chapters (3, 4) and the prescription (11) are the most immediately actionable. The evidence chapters (5–10) are what make the prescription credible.

---

*Mechanism definitions and the full diagnostic framework are in Chapter 4 and are not duplicated elsewhere in the report.*
