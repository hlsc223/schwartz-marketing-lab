The Durable Demand Framework

1
Diagnose the mechanism

Identify what is actually driving the traffic pattern before investing in recovery or growth.

↓
2
Build content that deserves a click

Invest in content whose value requires the reader to engage, not content that can be satisfied without a click.

↓
3
Build a portfolio of durable content

Spread growth across durable content types so no single page or content type dominates the program.

↓
4
Measure portfolio health

Track cannibalization, position consolidation, and traffic mix to tell whether the program is compounding or just replacing losses.

↓
5
Evaluate every investment with two questions

Does it create ongoing engagement, and can an AI system satisfy the query without a click?
