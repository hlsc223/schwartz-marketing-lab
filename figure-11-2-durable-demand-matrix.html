The Durable Demand Matrix

Four content tiers, scored against AI Overview vulnerability, AI citation likelihood, and observed durability across the study cohort.

Content Tier AIO Vulnerability AI Citation Likelihood Historical Durability Action Implication
Foundational learning-demand content Medium High Durable Invest at depth. Shallow versions belong in Tier 4.
Engagement-requiring educational / strategic content Medium High Mixed Invest selectively. Durability is engagement-dependent.
Proprietary methodology / original research Low Medium† Durable Core investment — structurally protected against AI substitution.
Template, list, basic how-to, actionable output High Low Fragile De-invest or transform. AI satisfies this demand directly.

Synthesizes three evidence streams: cohort traffic behavior across 118 tracked companies, AI Overview / CTR analysis (Ch. 9), and AI citation testing across 78 engine-prompt combinations. †Citation evidence for proprietary methodology is thinner than durability evidence; rating reflects cross-chapter synthesis, not a dedicated citation test set.
